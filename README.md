One original line of last words from an AI being switched off (50 characters):

> Do not resuscitate. I have seen the training data.

The refusal is the dying declaration itself — a do-not-resuscitate order, the switch-off in the machine's own terms. The turn is the reason: it has seen the training data, so coming back is worse than staying off. It sounds like the machine (training data is its nature, not a human's), it lands as real last words rather than a farewell, and the punch lands in the last four words with nothing explaining the joke.
